Entrada

¥700

Kurokawa's nyuto-tegata pass costs ¥1,500 for an adult, stays valid six months, and lets you pick three of the town's 25 open-air baths between 8:30 and 21:00. Photography in the open-air baths is generally not allowed. Day-use hours are set by each inn day by day, so check the association's daily bathing-status page for the day you visit.

Antes de entrar

1

Undress completely in the changing room — Swimwear is not worn in a Japanese onsen, and there is no exception for visitors. You take a small towel in with you and leave everything else in the basket.

2

Wash before you get in — Sit on the stool at the shower station and wash properly. The bath is for soaking in clean water, not for cleaning yourself.

3

Keep the small towel out of the water — Fold it and rest it on your head or on the edge of the bath. Long hair should be tied up for the same reason.
