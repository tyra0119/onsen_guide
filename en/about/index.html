About this site

A guide to Japanese hot springs for people arriving from outside Japan, built around one question: can you get in with a tattoo? Where there is no answer, this site says there is no answer.

Last updated · 2026-08-31

Where the answers come from

Every tattoo answer comes from what the facility itself publishes, or from a prefecture's official tourism site. Aggregator sites, reviews and search-result summaries are not used as sources. Each facility page names what was read.

"Unknown" does not mean nobody looked. It means it was looked for and not found. It does not mean you would be refused. It means the only way to be sure is to ring and ask.

Prices and opening hours are as they were on the day they were read. They change. Check with the facility before you travel.

If something here is wrong

Corrections from facilities are welcome. A policy has changed, a bath has closed, a sentence misrepresents you — any of those will be fixed. Reports from readers are just as welcome.

Write here. Corrections from facilities are checked and applied promptly. tyra.arimatsu@gmail.com

Where this site stands on advertising

The only thing this site has is that what it says is true. Advertising, if it appears, cannot be allowed to cost that. Four things are settled.

One: advertising will be marked as advertising. It will not be dressed up as a recommendation.

Two: advertising does not change the order facilities appear in. No facility moves up the page by paying.

Three: advertising has no bearing on what this site says about tattoos. That is decided by what the facility publishes, and by nothing else.

Four: no advertising sits next to the tattoo verdict or its notes. Nothing should be able to make you wonder, mid-sentence, whether the verdict was bought.

How it is built

A static site with no server-side code. Maps are OpenStreetMap; coordinates come from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan's address lookup. A pin marks where the address is, which can sit a hundred metres from the door.
