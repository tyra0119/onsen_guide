Privacy

This site has no accounts, no logins and no forms. There is no mechanism here that could receive your name or your email address, because none was ever built. Even so, opening any web page sends something somewhere. This page says what, and to whom, without rounding it down.

Last updated · 2026-08-31

What stays on your device

Your saved baths, and the record of which facilities you have already opened, are written to your own browser and nowhere else. They are not sent to a server. There is no way for us to read them.

Clearing your browsing data clears them. A private window discards them when you close it. They do not follow you to another device.

What is sent when a page loads

The site is a set of static files, served by GitHub Pages. Opening a page tells the host your IP address and what browser you are using. That is how the web works and no site avoids it.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address as well. Map tiles come from OpenStreetMap, so opening the map does the same for their tile servers.

Sorting by distance uses your location only when you ask it to. That position is used for the arithmetic inside your browser and is not sent anywhere.

Analytics

Where analytics are used at all, only a cookieless kind is used. Individual visitors are not identified, and nothing follows you across other sites. What it produces is a count — how many times a page was read — and nothing that points back to a person.

This build runs no analytics at all. No measurement script is loaded.

Advertising

If advertising ever appears here it will be labelled as advertising. It will never affect the order facilities are shown in, and it will never affect what this site says about tattoos. The reasoning is set out on the About page.

When this page changes

If the handling of analytics or advertising changes, this page is changed first. What is written above is what is actually running.
