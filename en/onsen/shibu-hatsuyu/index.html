Hatsu-yu (First Bath)

一番湯 初湯 · Shibu Onsen, Nagano

The first of Shibu's nine bathhouses. Sodium and calcium sulfate-chloride, weakly acidic and hot, and said to be good for the stomach and gut. On the round of the nine you stamp a prayer cloth at each bath. With all nine, you climb to the Shibu Koyakushi shrine above the town for the last stamp, and the vow is complete: the nine — a homophone for hardship — are washed away, and what is asked for is protection, safe childbirth and long life.

Before you go in

1

Undress completely in the changing room — Swimwear is not worn in a Japanese onsen, and there is no exception for visitors. You take a small towel in with you and leave everything else in the basket.

2

Wash before you get in — Sit on the stool at the shower station and wash properly. The bath is for soaking in clean water, not for cleaning yourself.

3

Keep the small towel out of the water — Fold it and rest it on your head or on the edge of the bath. Long hair should be tied up for the same reason.
