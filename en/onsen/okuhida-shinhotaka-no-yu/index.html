Shinhotaka-no-yu

新穂高の湯 · Okuhida Onsen-go, Gifu

The only mixed open-air bath among the day-use places here, beside the clear Gamata river with the gorge open in front of it. It is lit up when the new leaves come.

No tattoo policy published

We have not researched this yet. Neither the facility nor the Okuhida tourism association publishes anything about tattoos. That is not the same as having no rule — call ahead before you visit.

The bath

Mixed bathing, with separate changing rooms. A swimsuit or a bathing garment is required; the garment is sold for ¥350 at Higaku-no-yu and at Nakazaki Sanso.

  • Simple alkaline — Gentle on the skin; good for a first visit.

Before you go in

1

Undress completely in the changing room — Swimwear is not worn in a Japanese onsen, and there is no exception for visitors. You take a small towel in with you and leave everything else in the basket.

2

Wash before you get in — Sit on the stool at the shower station and wash properly. The bath is for soaking in clean water, not for cleaning yourself.

3

Keep the small towel out of the water — Fold it and rest it on your head or on the edge of the bath. Long hair should be tied up for the same reason.
